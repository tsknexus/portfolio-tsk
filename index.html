Materials & files

Choosing the right material

Most people aren't sure which one they need — that's normal. Say what the part has to do and the right choice gets recommended for you.

PLA

The everyday choice — sharp detail, bright colours, excellent surface finish.

  • Keychains, gifts, figurines
  • Display & scale models
  • Not for hot cars or direct sun

PETG

Tougher and heat-resistant, with a slight gloss. The workhorse for real-world parts.

  • Brackets, mounts, enclosures
  • Outdoor & vehicle use
  • Items that take knocks

TPU (flexible)

Rubber-like and grippy — bends and springs back instead of snapping.

  • Grips, bumpers, gaskets
  • Phone & camera padding
  • Shock-absorbing parts
Have a 3D file? Accepted formats: .STL.3MF.OBJ.STEP — no file yet? A photo or hand sketch works just as well.
